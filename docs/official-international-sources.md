# Official international tender sources

## Australia — AusTender

- Current approaches to market: https://tenders.gov.au/atm
- Official homepage: https://www.tenders.gov.au/
- Public RSS feed used by the connector: https://tenders.gov.au/public_data/rss/rss.xml
- Official help/glossary: https://help.tenders.gov.au/glossary

The official page describes an Approach to Market as a notice inviting suppliers to participate in procurement, including tenders, quotes, expressions of interest, requests for information, and proposals. The connector reads only the official RSS feed, stores the official AusTender link, and does not submit bids or bypass registration requirements.
