5 Verification of the Worked Example

The figures on this sheet come from the worked example in Guide TP-G1: a 12.0 × 10.0 m residential building on a 6.0 m grid, 16 footings, 32 columns, three suspended slabs. Two independent checks:

Check 1 — concrete by element

Blinding 1.70×1.70×0.10×164.624 m³
Footings 1.50×1.50×0.40×1614.400 m³
Tie beams 0.30×0.60×8815.840 m³
Columns 0.30×0.40×3.20×3212.288 m³
Beams 0.25×0.50×88×222.000 m³
Slabs 12×10×0.15×354.000 m³
Sum123.15 m³

Check 2 — reinforcement per cubic metre

11.248 t over 123.15 m³ of structural concrete (excluding blinding, which is unreinforced) gives 91 kg/m³. For a residential frame this sits inside the normal 90–130 kg/m³ band — low but not implausible for light slabs on a regular grid.

A result outside 80–150 kg/m³ on a building of this type means the schedule has been misread, a diameter omitted, or laps left out. Use this ratio as a red flag, never as the quantity.
