5 Certificates and References

  • Certificates of completion, taking-over or substantial completion for each project cited — not merely a contract award letter.
  • Client satisfaction or performance letters where available; a reference letter that names the project, the value and the completion date is worth more than one that does not.
  • Valid ISO certificates with accreditation body and expiry date, plus the scope statement — a certificate whose scope does not cover building construction proves nothing.
  • Contractor classification and trade licence, current and legible.
