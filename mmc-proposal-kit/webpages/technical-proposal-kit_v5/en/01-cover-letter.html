Subject: Submission of Technical and Financial Proposal for   — Tender No.  

Dear Sir / Madam,

Having examined the Instructions to Bidders, the General and Particular Conditions of Contract, the Specifications, the Bill of Quantities, the Drawings and all addenda issued in respect of the above tender, and having visited the site and obtained all information necessary for the preparation of this offer, we, the undersigned, submit our technical and financial proposal for the execution and completion of the Works and the remedying of any defects therein.

We confirm that we have read and understood the complete tender documents, including every addendum and clarification issued, and that this offer is based on a full understanding of the scope of work and of the site conditions prevailing at the location of the Works.

We undertake to execute and complete the Works in accordance with the Specifications, Drawings and Bill of Quantities, to the quality standard required, for the total tender sum and within the programme stated in the financial offer and the time schedule appended hereto, and in any event within the period stated in the Instructions to Bidders.

We confirm that all documents and information submitted with this offer are true and accurate, and that any misstatement or concealment entitles the Employer to reject the offer or to take such other action as the contract permits.

This offer shall remain valid for the period stated in Section 2 above from the deadline for submission, and we undertake to furnish a performance security in the amount and form and on the terms set out in the Conditions of Contract should the award be made to us.

We understand that the Employer is not bound to accept the lowest or any offer, and that no cost incurred in the preparation of this offer will be reimbursed.

We remain at your disposal for any clarification or clarification meeting you consider appropriate.

Yours faithfully,
