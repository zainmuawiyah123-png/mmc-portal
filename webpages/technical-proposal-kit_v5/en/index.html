Two things you must change before submitting

The rates. Every unit rate in TP-09 is illustrative — it demonstrates the arithmetic and is not a market price. Replace all twenty-four with live supplier or subcontract quotations.
The people and projects. Every CV, client and reference in TP-06 and TP-07 must be real and verifiable. A reference the evaluator cannot reach is worse than no reference.

03 Using the Pages

  • Type directly into any field. Entries save to your own browser as you type; nothing is transmitted anywhere.
  • Print or save as PDF from the button in the top bar. A4, with background graphics enabled.
  • Switch currency between USD, JOD, SAR and AED in the top bar. Conversion happens at display time, so nothing you typed is lost.
  • Edit a measurement or a rate on TP-09 or TP-14 and every dependent total recalculates.
