Resource Plan: Organisation, Key Personnel, Equipment and Materials

The evaluator is asking one question: can this bidder actually staff, plant and supply this project? Named people, owned plant and identified sources answer it; general assurances do not.

What earns marks: staff named with qualifications and years of experience, plant listed as owned or leased with capacity, and material sources named with an approved-supplier route. A CV that says “civil engineer” without a registration number is a CV nobody can score.
