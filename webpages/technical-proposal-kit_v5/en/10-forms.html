Before you seal the envelope

Count the copies, confirm the original is marked as such, check every signature and stamp, verify that the pricing in words and figures agree, and confirm the submission address and time. Late is late — there is no discretion, and a courier timestamp is not the Employer's clock.

RFI discipline

One question per RFI. A document with six questions gets one answer to three of them. Closing date for questions is stated in the instructions — an RFI raised after that date will not be answered and its subject becomes your risk at the price you bid.

How to make a VE proposal acceptable

A value engineering proposal is accepted on three conditions: it does not reduce performance or design life, it carries an equivalent or better warranty, and the saving is shared or passed on as the contract requires. A proposal that offers a saving but weakens the specification will be rejected and will damage the credibility of your other submissions.
