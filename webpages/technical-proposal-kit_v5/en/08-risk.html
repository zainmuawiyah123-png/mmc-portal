Scoring logic: each risk needs an owner, a trigger that tells you it is happening, a mitigation that acts before the impact, and a contingency with a value and a time allowance. “Monitor closely” is not a mitigation.

1 Risk Assessment Methodology

Risk score = likelihood × consequence, each scored 1–5. Scores are reviewed at the weekly progress meeting and any movement of two points or more is reported to the project director.

4 Risk Management During Execution

  • Weekly: register reviewed at the progress meeting; new risks added, closed risks archived with the reason for closure.
  • Monthly: risk report to the Employer identifying movement, mitigation status and any risk that has become an issue requiring a decision.
  • Early warning: any event likely to affect time or cost is notified in writing immediately, with a contemporaneous record — not reconstructed at the end of the project.
  • Escalation: a critical risk is escalated to the project director within 24 hours, with a recommendation and the cost of each option.
