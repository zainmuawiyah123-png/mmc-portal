The quantities are not illustrative: they are derived from the declared geometry of the worked example and can be verified by hand from the dimensions in each row.

Consistency checks before submission

  • Group subtotals here must equal the Summary sheet totals in Form TP-14.
  • The offer value must match the figure written in the financial offer and the cover letter.
  • Payment percentages in TP-03 must be defensible from these group shares.
  • Any group above 20% of the total deserves a second look at its rates.
