1 HSE Policy and Responsibilities

No activity on this project is worth an injury. Every person on site has the authority to stop work they consider unsafe, without needing permission and without recrimination, and the stop-work record is reviewed by the project manager the same day rather than quietly buried.

2 Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRA)

Risk score = likelihood × severity on a 5×5 matrix. Residual risk is the score after the stated control is in place. Any residual score of 15 or above requires a written safe system of work approved by the project manager before the activity starts.

4 Permit to Work System

No permit, no work. Permits are issued by the HSE officer for one shift only, are displayed at the work face, and are closed out by the issuer after inspection — not left to expire unattended.

5 Training and Awareness

  • Site induction before first entry, valid for the project, covering site rules, emergency arrangements, welfare and reporting. No induction, no access — enforced at the gate.
  • Task-specific training for scaffolding, lifting, confined space, electrical work and plant operation, with certificates held on file and expiry dates tracked.
  • Toolbox talks weekly, delivered by the supervisor at the work face, on the hazard of the week — not a repeat of the induction.
  • Emergency drills every three months: evacuation, confined-space rescue and first aid response.
