Retention and release

Retention is deducted at the stated rate from each interim payment until the cap stated in the contract is reached. Half is released on the certificate of substantial completion; the balance on the certificate of completion of the defects liability period, against the maintenance certificate.

5 Schedule Control

  • Weekly: actual versus planned progress by activity, with the physical percentage measured against the agreed weighting — not against the contractor's own estimate.
  • Monthly: updated programme issued with the interim payment application, showing the critical path and any float consumed.
  • On any delay event: notice within the period stated in the contract, with a contemporaneous record. Delay noticed months later is usually lost regardless of cause.
  • Recovery: a written recovery plan is issued within seven days of the programme showing negative float, naming the additional resource and the cost of that resource.
