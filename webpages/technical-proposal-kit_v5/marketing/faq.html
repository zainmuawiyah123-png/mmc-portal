Everything contractors ask before subscribing

Grouped by topic. If something is missing, the Enterprise onboarding call exists to fill the gap.

Getting started

What exactly am I buying?

A set of HTML documents that run in your browser: thirteen tender forms, two calculation sheets and two guides. They are fillable, printable and save your entries locally as you type. Professional and Enterprise also include the Excel version of the quantity take-off.

Do I need to install anything?

No. There is no desktop application, no plugin and no account to create on the form pages. You open the document, type into it, and print. This is also why it works on a site laptop with no admin rights.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes — the full English kit is open for preview. Print a form, use the take-off sheet, and decide from the actual output rather than from a marketing page.

The forms and the numbers

Are the prices in the example real?

No. The unit rates in the price schedule are illustrative and the document states this on the page. They exist to demonstrate the arithmetic chain from take-off to offer value. Replace every rate with a live supplier or subcontract quotation before you submit a real tender. The quantities are different: they are derived from the declared geometry of the worked example and can be verified by hand from the dimensions in each row.

How does the take-off stay consistent with the price schedule?

The same figures are published in both places and there is an explicit cross-check block. If you change a dimension, the take-off recalculates; the price schedule rows carry the same rates. The usage guide's fifteen-point review starts with the requirement that the two totals agree to the cent.

Can I change the currency?

Yes — USD, JOD, SAR and AED, switched from the top bar on any page. Conversion happens at display time at fixed reference rates, so nothing you typed is destroyed when you switch. Edit the rates in assets/en.js if you need to track market rates.

I edited a dimension and the total did not change.

Check that you edited a measurement field (L, W, H, No. or waste), not the calculated quantity cell. Calculated columns are outputs, not inputs. If the total still does not move, the file was opened in a viewer that blocks scripts — open it in a normal browser.

Subscription and billing

Use an international bank transfer to the same Jordanian IBAN. Contact us before sending the payment to receive the applicable SWIFT/BIC and correspondent-bank details, confirm the invoice currency and amount, and ensure that transfer fees are covered by the sender.

Is it monthly or annual?

Either. Annual billing is ten months' price, which is the same as two months free — about a 17% discount. Switch between the two with the toggle on the pricing page.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Access runs to the end of the paid period. Documents you have already printed or exported as PDF remain yours.

What happens if I move from Starter to Professional mid-term?

The remaining balance is credited against the new plan and the term restarts from the change date. Nothing you have already filled in is lost — the forms are the same documents; the plan only changes which editions and features you can open.

Do you offer a discount for a single-project use?

Starter is the single-project answer: three active projects, one seat, one language. If you need only one tender, Starter for one month is cheaper than any one-off licence we would have to administer.

Data, privacy and offline use

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser's local storage, on your own machine. The form pages have no backend, no account and no analytics. Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere. This is deliberate: tender pricing is commercially sensitive and contractors should not be uploading it to a tool they do not control.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the files are on your machine. There is no server call required to fill, calculate or print.

What if I clear my browser data?

Your saved entries are lost — they live in local storage. Print or export the PDF before clearing, and keep the PDF as the record. This is the one behaviour worth telling every user about up front.

Editions and support

How complete is the Arabic edition?

It is a full RTL version of all thirteen documents with the same calculations and the same cross-checks — not a summary or a translated cover. It is included in Professional and Enterprise.

Can I put my company logo on the forms?

On Professional and Enterprise. The cover and header are the two places that matter; the body layout stays fixed so that the printed page remains legible.

Do you update the forms when codes change?

Yes, and that is what the subscription funds. Enterprise subscribers also receive the quarterly code-change brief. Corrections are versioned, so you can see what changed between the version you printed and the current one.

Can you build forms to our own tender format?

On Enterprise. Send the format and the fields, and the forms are rebuilt to it with the same calculation layer underneath.

One honest caveat

This kit is a document system, not an estimating platform. It will produce a consistent, printable, internally reconciling technical submission. It will not tell you what a cubic metre of concrete costs in your city — that number comes from your suppliers, and the forms are built on the assumption that you will put it there yourself.
