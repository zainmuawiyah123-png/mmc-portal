About MMC

One platform. Multiple construction services.

MMC is an independent digital ecosystem designed to connect organisations and professionals across the construction and procurement value chain. Our goal is to make opportunities, information and collaboration easier to access.

Our direction

The construction ecosystem, more connected.

We are bringing practical digital services together for procurement entities, contractors, consultants and project teams across Jordan, the region and international markets.
